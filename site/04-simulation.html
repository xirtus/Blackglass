04 / SYSTEMS

World Simulation

The game must generate people who appear to have lived before the mission started: routines, relationships, devices, locations, habits and history. The simulation needs enough internal consistency for the player to reason from it.

DETERMINISTIC SEEDSECS / DATA-ORIENTEDLOD SIMULATION

Authoritative simulation layers

Physical world

People, vehicles, buildings, roads, schedules, gatherings, transit, weather and infrastructure states.

Social world

Relationships, trust, contact frequency, shared history, work groups, families, media circles and online communities.

Information world

Archive fragments, copies, derivatives, credibility, holders, transfers, mirrors, public awareness and demand.

Generated life generator

Important agents are generated from a deterministic scenario seed. The same seed must recreate their household, job, recurring locations, devices, friendships and recent event history. This permits reproducible QA and allows the game to generate convincing timelines before the leak begins.

Perspective and controller layer

The world simulation is shared. A strict perspective policy decides which fields become visible to the human UI or opposing AI. BLACKGLASS receives observations, confidence and inferred associations; HYDRA receives its own held copies, intentions and perceived relationships plus intentionally fuzzy state-pressure signals. Neither side may read hidden ground truth through UI selectors.

Perspective = BLACKGLASS | HYDRA | OMNISCIENT_REPLAY
Controller = Human | BoundedAI
selectVisibleState(simulation, perspective, controlledEntities) → ViewModel

When the human chooses HYDRA, a bounded BLACKGLASS AI must use the same Watch Index, observation stream, finite resources and intervention rules available to a human containment player. Difficulty improves calibration and attention rather than granting secret knowledge.

Simulation level of detail

LODWhen usedRepresentation
LOD 0 — HeroSelected / nearby important actorsFull schedule, route, animation, devices, detailed decision state.
LOD 1 — LocalVisible city actorsRoad/pedestrian graph movement, simplified decisions, event generation.
LOD 2 — RegionalOffscreen known contactsDiscrete travel/contact events; no continuous rendering.
LOD 3 — AggregateLarge populationsCompartment/network statistics and sampled representative nodes.
LOD 4 — GlobalViral stateCity/server/network aggregates plus a small set of explicit high-value nodes.

Traffic and crowds

Road traffic should be graph-driven rather than rigid-body simulated. Vehicles occupy routes and contribute to congestion density. Intersections have signal phases. A blackout or grid intervention can change signal state and create emergent congestion without simulating every bumper collision. Pedestrian crowds use zones and path graphs; only selected agents need high-detail animation.

Determinism contract

Scenario seed + player decisions + versioned rules must reproduce the same systemic run. Use named RNG streams per subsystem so adding a cosmetic random draw does not reshuffle social graphs or leak outcomes.
