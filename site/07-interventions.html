Intervention System

Interventions are tactical and strategic levers with prerequisites, delays, uncertainty and side effects. Real-world harmful methods stay abstract; classified black-program technology can become intentionally extravagant.

Opportunity windows

Many actions should require abstract opportunity conditions rather than detailed real procedures. A field intervention may require “asset in range,” “identity confidence above threshold,” “legal authority,” “low civilian density,” or “communication link available.” This creates planning without turning the game into a procedural real-world harm manual.

HYDRA counterplay actions

The disclosure side receives its own data-driven command catalog. Actions are intentionally high-level and resolve through character traits, trust, time and world state rather than teaching real operational evasion.

Design rule: action creates a new puzzle

Never implement “click target → problem deleted.” A blackout stops a connection but destroys observation. A detention can trigger panic sharing. A server shutdown may reveal backup mirrors. A spectacular strike can remove a node while making the entire world ask why it happened.
