Maps, Locations & Scale

Real geographic structure provides grounding, while mission-sensitive private details are fictionalized. Rendering technology changes with scale so the same simulation can move from a city block to Earth without forcing one renderer to do everything.

OSM FOUNDATIONMAPLIBRE3 SCALE MODES

Location philosophy

Use real public geography where it improves orientation: streets, public government complexes, city structure, airports, coastlines and infrastructure categories. For private residences or sensitive nonpublic spaces, use fictionalized estates/interiors rather than reproducing real addresses, floor plans or security layouts.

Local leak-point templates

Government complex

Dense access control, officials, staff, contractors, tourists, press and strong surveillance coverage.

Archive / courthouse

Physical documents, staff routines, legal actors, media attention and multiple exits.

Contractor campus

Employees, devices, parking, badge logs, remote access and corporate networks.

Airport / station

High mobility, crowds, handoff opportunities and sudden jurisdiction changes.

Data center

Few physical actors but high network amplification and infrastructure dependencies.

Fictional private estate

Low population, private security, household/staff social graph; intentionally not an accurate real residence.

Geographic asset pipeline

OpenStreetMap / permitted geographic data ├── roads / intersections ──► route graph + traffic lanes ├── building footprints ─────► extrusion / map3d / GLB cache ├── landuse / parks ─────────► procedural materials + Gaia / Dryad ├── water / coast ───────────► map surface + Poseidon where close-up matters └── POI categories ──────────► scenario anchors, not trusted as secure-layout truth

Perspective-specific map intelligence

BLACKGLASS can layer synthetic observations, coverage estimates, target trails and infrastructure controls over the geographic world. HYDRA uses the same geography but sees only its controlled characters, known contacts, committed destinations, public events and deliberately uncertain pressure indicators. This makes identical streets feel strategically different without maintaining two maps.

Camera behavior

At city scale, use a near-orthographic or controlled perspective map camera with smooth zoom, pitch and target follow. Selected agents get selection rings, trail history and prediction cones. Zooming out replaces models with symbols rather than shrinking full meshes indefinitely. The transition should feel like the same system increasing abstraction.

Destruction and intervention states

Do not require bespoke destroyed variants for every structure. Use procedural state overlays: power-off emissive changes, smoke/fire VFX, blocked-road decals, connectivity masks, crowd density and simplified destruction proxies. Only hero locations need authored damage states.
