Surveillance & Watch Index

Surveillance is represented as synthetic observation streams with gaps, confidence and ownership ambiguity. The player builds a probabilistic picture from overlapping sensors rather than receiving perfect truth.

SYNTHETIC DATA ONLYCONFIDENCE EVERYWHEREWATCH CIRCLES

Observation sources

Use broad public concepts as game abstractions, not integrations with real surveillance networks. All events are generated by the game simulation.

Watch Index

The Watch Index replaces a flat “watch list.” The player watches circles around a subject: family, work, media, old friends, online groups, professional associations and unknown-contact clusters. Watching an entire circle costs more than watching a person but can catch indirect transfers.

Surveillance pressure from the HYDRA side

The disclosure player must never receive a literal map of hidden cameras, exact Watch Index priority or unseen observations. Instead the system exposes fictionalized game feedback: a fuzzy heat estimate, visible interventions, contact concern, public/legal pressure, missing observations, infrastructure changes and other consequences. The uncertainty is the mechanic.

HYDRA indicatorRepresentsNot shown
HeatEstimated scrutiny around the controlled branch.Exact sensor count or state confidence.

Design boundary: this is a strategy abstraction, not a practical real-world guide for surveillance evasion.

Observation confidence

Every observation has a source, timestamp, confidence and decay. “Vehicle 41 was seen at intersection X at 14:31” is not identical to “Person Maya was there.” The system must distinguish object identification, ownership inference and occupant inference.

Confidence sourceExample uncertainty
Direct recognitionImage quality, occlusion, synthetic false matches.
Vehicle associationBorrowed/shared vehicle or stale registration.
Device associationShared device, old device, secondary account.
IP observationCoarse geography, shared network, VPN-like abstraction.
Social inferenceHigh message frequency does not prove a transfer.
Behavioral anomalyRoutine deviation may be benign.

Anomaly engine

The anomaly engine compares current events to the synthetic baseline rather than relying on magic labels. It should surface interpretable features: “left work 2h early,” “contacted dormant friend,” “travel mode changed,” “device went silent,” or “unusual file-transfer-sized event.” The score is a hint, not truth.

Analyst board

Players can pin nodes and draw hypothesis links. A hypothesis can be evaluated by multiple fictional models that disagree. This is the core detective layer: the player decides which model to trust and where to spend attention.

ATHENA

Strong on routine and movement; can overvalue behavioral anomalies.

ORACLE

Strong on social graph and trust; weaker when unknown nodes dominate.

JANUS

Conservative model focused on missing explanations; frequently returns UNKNOWN.
