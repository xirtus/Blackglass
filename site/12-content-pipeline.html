Content & Data Pipeline

Scenarios should be authored as data. Designers define a leak origin, important actors, constraints and scripted beats; the generator fills the surrounding world with coherent generated lives.

PLACE HEROESApply authored actors and story constraints.

Procedural caches

Generated map chunks, building meshes, vegetation seeds and population identity seeds should be cached by content hash. The cache stores reproducible results, not mutable gameplay state. This makes development reloads fast while preserving deterministic generation.

Narrative content

Dialogue should be sparse and mostly delivered through alert text, analyst notes, authorization messages, intercepted-public-content abstractions and mission briefings. The deadpan procedural voice is part of the aesthetic. Avoid relying on long cutscenes that interrupt the dashboard fantasy.

Mod support path

Keep scenario manifests, classified programs, asset registries and localization strings external enough that future community scenarios can be added without modifying the simulation core. A later editor can expose map center, hero placement, leak parameters, surveillance density and scripted beats.
